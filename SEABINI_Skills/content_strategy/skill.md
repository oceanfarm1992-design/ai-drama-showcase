# SEABINI Content Strategy Skill

## Content pillars

### Songs

Original repeatable preschool songs.

### Stories

Character-driven ocean adventures.

### Discovery

Simple facts and questions about marine life and the ocean.

### Kindness

Friendship, helping others, caring for animals and caring for the
environment.

## Content mix

Maintain a balance between entertainment-led stories and simple
educational discovery. Do not turn every video into a lesson.

## Recurring formats

-   3--6 minute episodes
-   short songs
-   Shorts
-   compilations
-   bedtime episodes
-   themed collections

## Season planning

Start with a small pilot set. Identify which characters, topics, songs
and story structures perform best before scaling production.

## Topic selection

Prioritize topics that are: - visually interesting - easy to
understand - emotionally engaging - repeatable - scientifically sound -
connected to the SEABINI world

## Differentiation

SEABINI should feel like an ocean adventure universe, not a generic
nursery-rhyme channel with fish added to the background.
