# SEABINI World-Building Skill

## Core setting

Rainbow Reef is the primary home base for Bini and friends.

## Reusable locations

1.  Rainbow Reef --- colorful home base.
2.  Mangrove Bay --- shallow water and mangrove adventures.
3.  Seagrass Garden --- calm habitat for small marine life.
4.  Shell Beach --- beach and tide-zone stories.
5.  Blue Lagoon --- open swimming and racing.
6.  The Old Wreck --- mystery and exploration.
7.  Moonlight Reef --- calm nighttime and bedtime stories.

## Environment rules

-   Bright, welcoming and visually readable.
-   Use rounded, appealing shapes.
-   Keep important characters clearly separated from backgrounds.
-   Reuse environments intelligently for production efficiency.
-   Add environmental details without clutter.

## Educational accuracy

When real marine biology is referenced, prefer scientifically correct
concepts. Simplify explanations for preschool comprehension without
teaching false facts.

## Story opportunities

Each environment should support songs, discovery, friendship problems,
counting, colors, animal behavior and simple conservation lessons.
