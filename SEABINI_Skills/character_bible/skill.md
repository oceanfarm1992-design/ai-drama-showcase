# SEABINI Character Bible Skill

## Main cast

### Bini --- The Explorer

Curious, brave, kind, playful and always asking questions. Signature
behavior: notices something unusual and investigates. Signature phrase:
"Let's dive in!"

### Tula --- The Turtle

Calm, wise, patient and thoughtful. Role: slows the group down and
encourages good decisions.

### Ollo --- The Octopus

Clever, creative, funny and inventive. Role: solves problems using eight
arms and unusual ideas.

### Dodo --- The Dolphin

Energetic, confident, sporty and adventurous. Role: creates movement,
excitement and playful challenges.

### Pipi --- The Little Fish

Sweet, curious and slightly shy. Role: asks simple questions that
younger children may also have.

## Visual consistency

Characters must retain the same: - body proportions - face and eye
design - colors - signature features - silhouette - age impression -
animation style

Do not redesign a recurring character for convenience.

## Interaction rule

Characters must have contrasting personalities. Avoid making everyone
equally loud, happy, clever or adventurous.

## Character development

Each story should allow at least one character trait to matter to the
plot. Characters may make small, harmless mistakes and learn from them.

## Safety

Avoid graphic danger, frightening predators, realistic injury, cruelty
or situations inappropriate for ages 2--6.
