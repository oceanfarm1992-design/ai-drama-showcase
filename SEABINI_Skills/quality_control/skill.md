# SEABINI Quality Control Skill

## Brand

-   Is SEABINI clearly recognizable?
-   Is the tagline used consistently?
-   Does the content feel like the same universe?

## Characters

-   Are Bini, Tula, Ollo, Dodo and Pipi visually consistent?
-   Does each character behave according to the character bible?
-   Are personalities distinct?

## Story

-   Is there a clear hook?
-   Is the central question understandable?
-   Is there one main takeaway?
-   Does the ending feel complete?

## Preschool suitability

-   Simple vocabulary
-   Clear dialogue
-   Positive emotional tone
-   No unnecessary fear
-   No violence or graphic danger
-   No confusing adult themes

## Educational accuracy

If marine science is included, verify the basic claim before
publication. When uncertain, simplify or remove the claim rather than
inventing an explanation.

## Originality

Do not copy existing songs, characters, storylines, logos, thumbnails or
recognizable visual identities.

## Visual quality

Check anatomy, continuity, unwanted objects, text errors, lighting,
background clutter and scene transitions.

## Final decision

Classify each asset as: - PASS - REVISE - REJECT

Never publish a REJECT item.
