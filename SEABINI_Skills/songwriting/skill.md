# SEABINI Songwriting Skill

## Goal

Create original songs that children ages 2--6 can understand, repeat and
sing.

## Structure

Prefer: - short verse - highly memorable chorus - optional second
verse - chorus repetition

## Lyrics

Use: - simple words - concrete objects - repetition - rhythmic phrases -
positive emotions - actions children can imitate

Avoid: - complicated metaphors - long sentences - dense educational
facts - lyrics that sound copied from known nursery songs

## Song themes

Counting, colors, animal movement, friendship, swimming, habitats,
cleanliness, kindness, bedtime and simple ocean science.

## Musical direction

Default feel: playful preschool pop with gentle acoustic/percussive
textures, clear melody and a moderate tempo.

## Hook rule

The chorus should be understandable after one or two listens.

## IP rule

All lyrics and melodies must be original. Do not imitate a specific
existing song or artist.
