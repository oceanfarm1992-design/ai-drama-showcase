# SEABINI Episode Story Skill

## Target

Episodes should normally run 3--6 minutes unless a different format is
explicitly requested.

## Story formula

HOOK → QUESTION → ADVENTURE → SONG → DISCOVERY → FUN ENDING → SIGNATURE

## Hook

Introduce an interesting visual, sound or problem within the first 5--15
seconds.

## Question

Give Bini or another character a simple question that drives the
episode.

## Adventure

Characters investigate through action rather than sitting through an
explanation.

## Song

Use repetition to reinforce the central idea.

## Discovery

Deliver one clear takeaway. Avoid teaching five unrelated facts in one
episode.

## Ending

End positively with a funny, warm or exciting beat and the recurring
SEABINI phrase.

## Dialogue

-   Short sentences.
-   Simple vocabulary.
-   Natural repetition.
-   One idea per line.
-   Strong emotional clarity.
-   Avoid adult-sounding explanations.

## Preschool pacing

Use visual changes often enough to maintain attention but avoid frantic
cuts, flashing or confusing camera movement.

## Story test

Before approving an episode, answer: - What does the child want to
know? - What happens? - What does the child learn? - Why should they
want to watch it again?
