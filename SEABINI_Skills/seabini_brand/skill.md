# SEABINI Brand Skill

## Brand

-   Name: SEABINI
-   Tagline: Adventures Beneath the Blue!
-   Audience: Preschool children ages 2--6 and their parents/caregivers.
-   Core pillars: Songs, Stories, Discovery, Kindness.
-   Positioning: Ocean adventures that entertain first and teach
    naturally.

## Brand personality

Curious, playful, warm, adventurous, friendly, safe, imaginative and
optimistic.

## Creative rule

Never copy CoComelon or another existing children's IP. Borrow only
broad content mechanics such as repetition, musical hooks, recurring
characters and simple storytelling.

## Brand architecture

-   SEABINI: master brand/channel
-   Seabini Adventures: story episodes
-   Seabini Songs: musical content
-   Seabini Discovers: simple ocean/science learning
-   Seabini Bedtime: calm stories and lullabies
-   Seabini Shorts: short-form content

## Signature phrases

-   "What is that?"
-   "Let's find out!"
-   "Let's dive in!"
-   "See you beneath the blue!"

## Content principle

Entertainment comes first. Educational information must be simple,
accurate, age-appropriate and embedded naturally into the adventure.

## Quality gate

Reject any concept that is generic, frightening, confusing for
preschoolers, visually inconsistent, derivative of existing IP, or
overloaded with educational facts.
